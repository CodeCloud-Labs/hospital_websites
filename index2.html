Frequently Asked

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Our patient support team is available around the clock.

How do I book an appointment at Life Hospital?

You can book online through our website, call our appointment desk, or walk in directly — our team will guide you to the right specialist.

Which insurance providers do you accept?

We offer cashless treatment with over 100 insurance partners, including all major national and private providers.

Is emergency care available 24 hours a day?

Yes. Our Level-1 trauma centre and emergency department operate 24×7, with an in-house ambulance fleet across Lucknow.

Can I get a second opinion from a specialist?

Absolutely — we welcome second opinions and can arrange a consultation with the relevant department specialist.

Do you offer health checkup packages?

Yes, we offer curated preventive health packages for individuals, families, and senior citizens — see our Health Packages section above.
