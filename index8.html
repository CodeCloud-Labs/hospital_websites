Our Story

Twenty-five years of
healing, reimagined.

From a single ward in 1998 to a multi-specialty institution trusted by over a million lives — our journey has always centred on one idea: care that feels human, delivered with world-class precision.

1998
First hospital opens with 40 beds and a singular mission.
2006
Expansion into cardiac and maternal care centres.
2013
Modern infrastructure — new towers, ICUs, diagnostics wing.
2019
Advanced technology: AI diagnostics and robotic-assisted surgery.
Today
500+ beds, 120+ specialists, one uncompromising standard.
